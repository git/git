What's cooking in git.git (Oct 2026, #09)
-----------------------------------------

Here are the topics that have been cooking in my tree.  Commits
prefixed with '+' are in 'next' (being in 'next' is a sign that a
topic is stable enough to be used and is a candidate to be in a
future release).  Commits prefixed with '-' are only in 'seen', and
aren't considered "accepted" at all.  They may be annotated with a URL
to a message that raises issues but they are by no means exhaustive.
A topic without enough support may be discarded after a long period
of no activity (of course, it can be resubmitted when new interest
arises).

It is highly unusual to have 120 topic merges between 'master' and
'seen', with only 40 of them being in, or anywhere close to being
in, 'next'.  Many topics are left without attracting interest from
others.  Many others have not adequately addressed comments from
reviewers, stalling in the "Waiting for response" state.  Unless the
situation improves, I will more aggressively discard stalled topics.
I will also leave topics out of 'seen' until they receive some
reviews on the list.

Copies of the source code to Git live in many repositories, and the
following is a list of the ones I push into or their mirrors.  Some
repositories have only a subset of branches.
